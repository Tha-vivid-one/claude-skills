# Library (template)

The deep-study channel and gaps that keep coming back are the only times /debrief uses a
library. Copy this file to `library.md` and add sources you trust under each subject, one
per line. The subjects double as the topics Jev sorts moments into, so edit, add or remove
them to fit what you work on.

Item shape: `- [read] Title, Author · where to get it · part: the section to use · for: what it answers`.
Formats: read, watch, listen, do, reference. Put "start here" on the first pick for a subject.
Only add a source after checking it exists, and name a part only if you've seen it.

## statistics: Statistics and probability
Covers: sample size and noise, percentages and what they are out of, averages, probability, how confident to be, whether a difference is real, backtests, scoring rubrics and model scores, picking the best of many options.

## subscription_economics: Subscription business numbers
Covers: funnels and conversion rates, free trials, churn and retention, customer lifetime and lifetime value, customer acquisition cost and payback, MRR, revenue after app store fees, what a user is worth.

## writing: Writing
Covers: drafting in your own voice, clarity, editing, essays, posts, pitches, telling your own story, personal brand.

## user_research: User research and usability
Covers: talking to customers, interviews, usability tests, watching people use a product, onboarding friction, why users leave, surveys.

## design_systems: Design systems and accessibility
Covers: color palettes and tokens, greys and neutrals, color seeds and generated palettes, contrast and accessibility, type scales, components, platform guidelines, error, empty and loading states.

## marketing: Marketing: positioning, pricing, channels
Covers: positioning against competitors, pricing strategy and plan design, willingness to pay, acquisition channels, search ranking and SEO, ads and cost per install, messaging, engagement loops, competition and commoditization.

## small_company: Running a small company
Covers: founder basics, business models, what to prioritize, solo or tiny-team operations, startup advice.

## version_control: Git and version control
Covers: commits, branches, local vs remote, pushing and pulling, pull requests, merges, main vs other branches, worktrees, undoing mistakes.

## how_apps_fit: How apps fit together
Covers: servers, databases, APIs, webhooks, environment variables and config, deploys and hosting, builds, app signing and app stores, background jobs, page speed and web performance, architecture decision records (ADRs), what runs where.

## security_privacy: Security and privacy
Covers: logins and access control, API keys and secrets, what data is collected and consent, privacy law (GDPR, CCPA), honest claims in ads and copy, breaches.

## risk_decisions: Trading risk and decisions
Covers: stops and exits, position sizing, Kelly sizing, expectancy and R-multiples, discipline and tilt, judging a decision separately from its outcome, forecasting.

## market_mechanics: Market mechanics
Covers: fees, spreads, slippage, order types and fills, prediction market prices and edge, token supply and unlocks, chart indicators (moving averages, Bollinger Bands, MACD, RSI), behavioral finance.
